# Wave 984: a visible commitment to children

Israel asked for the public website to clearly show the 10% of profits commitment to Metro World Child and supplied its logo. The pledge already existed as a footer sentence. It is now a prominent, reusable section on the homepage after Our mission and on About Us before What we do, with a Giving back section link. The existing sentence remains verbatim from src/content/charity.ts.

The visual pairs a large percentage and original logo with a warm, light layout. Three native buttons explain everyday essentials, personal support, and learning and hope. Their content describes the charity's work, not measured IIG donation outcomes. The official programme, approach and sponsorship pages are the sources; source links and the UK website are available in the section. External links identify their new-tab behaviour. Keyboard selection, touch and reduced motion are supported.

The compact shared footer now includes the charity logo, the unchanged pledge, and a real link to the full About commitment. The supplied 80px image is used unchanged. The existing platform's 160px version of the same artwork is supplied as its high-density variant. No children photographs or invented impact totals are used.

The restored hero, Mission and About content, team portraits, candid photos, teal, role guides, FAQs, maps, legal text and signed-in platform are preserved. Charity content and controls were specified by docs/WAVE984_PLAN.md.
