Israel asked for the public website to clearly show the 10% of profits commitment to Metro World Child and supplied its logo. The pledge already existed as a footer sentence. It is now a prominent, reusable section on the homepage after Our mission and on About Us before What we do, with a Giving back section link. Their content describes the charity's work, not measured IIG donation outcomes. The official programme, approach and sponsorship pages are the sources; the user chose the Charity Commission record as the section destination. The existing platform's 160px version of the same artwork is supplied as its high-density variant. No children photographs or invented impact totals are used.

The restored hero, Mission and About content, team portraits, candid photos, teal, role guides, FAQs, maps, legal text and signed-in platform are preserved. Charity content and controls were specified by docs/WAVE984_PLAN.md.
