# Wave 1290: ambient Buddy and simpler chat

The owner rejected the separate Play-panel interpretation of wave 1279. This correction removes its Play, Pause, Resume, scene-selection and ball experience. Ask Buddy remains the entry to one conversation. The floating character performs occasional local scenes automatically while visible, with calm rests and a discreet hide/show control.

The conversation uses a Buddy-only header with a moving avatar and essential icon controls. A short greeting and compact example questions replace the category cards. The composer is smaller, and existing privacy and answer-limit notices remain available under About this chat. Suggestions continue to submit a question, and readiness, error, cancellation, focus and history behavior are preserved.

The animation correction uses the supplied character body, corrected hands and existing approved prop artwork. Five scene families have distinct variations: construction, dancing, peeking, stretching and polishing a house. Construction is driven by Buddy moving between masonry and fixture contacts with a rig-attached, left-held hammer. Wall courses, framed windows, roof and door assemble in sequence; dust covers the polished finish, and the door opens with a warm glow. Transforms begin with accelerating body rotation, hide the face on the rear half of a turn, conceal the shape change and decelerate into the new form. This is procedural canvas choreography using raster artwork, not a newly generated video.

The controller captures the displayed pose and position when a form change interrupts an act. A pending act and its remaining rest survive page-context changes. The transparent scene canvas extends left while the original character scale and click target stay fixed. Scenery loads shortly before the first act, independently of chat. Hidden/offscreen suspension, reduced motion and optional-art failure retain the ordinary character path.

Only the floating launcher opts into ambient scenery. Page-context form selection and ordinary chat-avatar behavior remain. Tailwind and bone-white tokens are retained. No backend, model-provider, secret, infrastructure or pricing change is included.
